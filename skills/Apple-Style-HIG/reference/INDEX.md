# Apple Human Interface Guidelines — Reference Index

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ (rendered from Apple's DocC JSON, crawled 2026-09-14). Each entry is a verbatim-text markdown rendering of one HIG page (images replaced by their alt text). Files live in `hig/<slug>.md`.

Lookup: `scripts/hig-lookup.sh <keyword>` in the Apple-Style skill, or open the file directly.

## Getting started

- [Design principles](hig/design-principles.md) — Explore fundamental principles that guide design across Apple platforms.
- [Designing for iOS](hig/designing-for-ios.md) — People depend on their iPhone to help them stay connected, play games, view media, accomplish tasks, and track personal data in any location and while on the go.
- [Designing for iPadOS](hig/designing-for-ipados.md) — People value the power, mobility, and flexibility of iPad as they enjoy media, play games, perform detailed productivity tasks, and bring their creations to life.
- [Designing for macOS](hig/designing-for-macos.md) — People rely on the power, spaciousness, and flexibility of a Mac as they perform in-depth productivity tasks, view media or content, and play games, often using several apps at once.
- [Designing for tvOS](hig/designing-for-tvos.md) — People enjoy the vibrant content, immersive experiences, and streamlined interactions that tvOS delivers in media and games, as well as in fitness, education, and home utility apps.
- [Designing for visionOS](hig/designing-for-visionos.md) — When people wear Apple Vision Pro, they enter an infinite 3D space where they can engage with your app or game while staying connected to their surroundings.
- [Designing for watchOS](hig/designing-for-watchos.md) — When people glance at their Apple Watch, they know they can access essential information and perform simple, timely tasks whether they’re stationary or in motion.
- [Designing for games](hig/designing-for-games.md) — When people play your game on an Apple device, they dive into the world you designed while relying on the platform features they love.
- [Designing for iPhone Duo](hig/designing-for-iphone-duo.md) — An app designed for iPhone Duo adapts seamlessly to both displays, providing a continuous experience as the device opens and closes.

## Foundations

- [Accessibility](hig/accessibility.md) — Accessible user interfaces empower everyone to have a great experience with your app or game.
- [App icons](hig/app-icons.md) — A unique, memorable icon expresses your app’s or game’s purpose and personality and helps people recognize it at a glance.
- [Branding](hig/branding.md) — Apps and games express their unique brand identity in ways that make them instantly recognizable while feeling at home on the platform and giving people a consistent experience.
- [Color](hig/color.md) — Judicious use of color can enhance communication, evoke your brand, provide visual continuity, communicate status and feedback, and help people understand information.
- [Dark Mode](hig/dark-mode.md) — Dark Mode is a systemwide appearance setting that uses a dark color palette to provide a comfortable viewing experience tailored for low-light environments.
- [Icons](hig/icons.md) — An effective icon is a graphic asset that expresses a single concept in ways people instantly understand.
- [Images](hig/images.md) — To make sure your artwork looks great on all devices you support, learn how the system displays content and how to deliver art at the appropriate scale factors.
- [Immersive experiences](hig/immersive-experiences.md) — In visionOS, you can design apps and games that extend beyond windows and volumes, immersing people in your content.
- [Inclusion](hig/inclusion.md) — Inclusive apps and games put people first by prioritizing respectful communication and presenting content and functionality in ways that everyone can access and understand.
- [Layout](hig/layout.md) — A consistent layout that adapts across display sizes, orientations, and multitasking configurations helps people understand and enjoy your app or game on all their devices.
- [Materials](hig/materials.md) — A material is a visual effect that creates a sense of depth, layering, and hierarchy between foreground and background elements.
- [Motion](hig/motion.md) — Beautiful, fluid motions bring the interface to life, conveying status, providing feedback and instruction, and enriching the visual experience of your app or game.
- [Privacy](hig/privacy.md) — Privacy is paramount: it’s critical to be transparent about the privacy-related data and resources you require and essential to protect the data people allow you to access.
- [Right to left](hig/right-to-left.md) — Support right-to-left languages like Arabic and Hebrew by reversing your interface as needed to match the reading direction of the related scripts.
- [SF Symbols](hig/sf-symbols.md) — SF Symbols provides thousands of consistent, highly configurable symbols that integrate seamlessly with the San Francisco system font, automatically aligning with text in all weights and sizes.
- [Spatial layout](hig/spatial-layout.md) — Spatial layout techniques help you take advantage of the infinite canvas of Apple Vision Pro and present your content in engaging, comfortable ways.
- [Typography](hig/typography.md) — Your typographic choices can help you display legible text, convey an information hierarchy, communicate important content, and express your brand or style.
- [Writing](hig/writing.md) — The words you choose within your app are an essential part of its user experience.

## Patterns

- [Charting data](hig/charting-data.md) — Presenting data in a chart can help you communicate information with clarity and appeal.
- [Collaboration and sharing](hig/collaboration-and-sharing.md) — Great collaboration and sharing experiences are simple and responsive, letting people engage with the content while communicating effectively with others.
- [Drag and drop](hig/drag-and-drop.md) — Using drag and drop, people can move or duplicate selected photos, text, and other content by dragging the selection from one location to another.
- [Entering data](hig/entering-data.md) — When you need information from people, design ways that make it easy for them to provide it without making mistakes.
- [Feedback](hig/feedback.md) — Feedback helps people know what’s happening, discover what they can do next, understand the results of actions, and avoid mistakes.
- [File management](hig/file-management.md) — Some apps can support documents and files that people expect to manage throughout the system.
- [Going full screen](hig/going-full-screen.md) — iPhone, iPad, and Mac offer full-screen modes that let people expand a window to fill the screen, hiding system controls and providing a distraction-free environment.
- [Launching](hig/launching.md) — A streamlined launch experience helps people start using your app or game immediately.
- [Live-viewing apps](hig/live-viewing-apps.md) — As you design a live-viewing app, prioritize the content and create fun, fluid interactions that encourage immersion in the live-viewing experience.
- [Loading](hig/loading.md) — The best content-loading experience finishes before people become aware of it.
- [Managing accounts](hig/managing-accounts.md) — When it doesn’t create an unnecessary barrier to your experience, an account can be a convenient way for people to access their content and track personal details.
- [Managing notifications](hig/managing-notifications.md) — Notifications can give people timely and important information, whether the device is locked or in use.
- [Modality](hig/modality.md) — Modality is a design technique that presents content in a separate, dedicated mode that prevents interaction with the parent view and requires an explicit action to dismiss.
- [Multitasking](hig/multitasking.md) — Multitasking lets people switch quickly from one app to another, performing tasks in each.
- [Offering help](hig/offering-help.md) — Although the most effective experiences are approachable and intuitive, you can provide contextual help when necessary.
- [Onboarding](hig/onboarding.md) — Onboarding can help people get a quick start using your app or game.
- [Playing audio](hig/playing-audio.md) — People expect rich audio experiences that automatically adjust when the context changes on the device.
- [Playing haptics](hig/playing-haptics.md) — Playing haptics can engage people’s sense of touch and bring their familiarity with the physical world into your app or game.
- [Playing video](hig/playing-video.md) — People expect to enjoy rich video experiences on their devices, regardless of the app or game they’re using.
- [Printing](hig/printing.md) — An iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or visionOS app can integrate system-provided print functionality when it makes sense, presenting custom printer- and document-specific options if necessary.
- [Ratings and reviews](hig/ratings-and-reviews.md) — People often view the ratings and reviews for an app or game before they download it.
- [Searching](hig/searching.md) — People use various search techniques to find content on their device, within an app, and within a document or file.
- [Settings](hig/settings.md) — People expect apps and games to just work, but they also appreciate having ways to customize the experience to fit their needs.
- [Undo and redo](hig/undo-and-redo.md) — Undo and redo gives people easy ways to reverse many types of actions, which can also help people explore and experiment safely as they learn a new interface or task.
- [Workouts](hig/workouts.md) — A great workout or fitness experience encourages people to engage with their current activity and helps them track their progress on their devices.

## Components


### Content  (`hig/content.md`)

- [Charts](hig/charts.md) — Organize data in a chart to communicate information with clarity and visual appeal.
- [Image views](hig/image-views.md) — An image view displays a single image — or in some cases, an animated sequence of images — on a transparent or opaque background.
- [Text views](hig/text-views.md) — A text view displays multiline, styled text content, which can optionally be editable.
- [Web views](hig/web-views.md) — A web view loads and displays rich web content, such as embedded HTML and websites, directly within your app.

### Layout And Organization  (`hig/layout-and-organization.md`)

- [Boxes](hig/boxes.md) — A box creates a visually distinct group of logically related information and components.
- [Collections](hig/collections.md) — A collection manages an ordered set of content and presents it in a customizable and highly visual layout.
- [Column views](hig/column-views.md) — A column view — also called a  — lets people view and navigate a data hierarchy using a series of vertical columns.
- [Disclosure controls](hig/disclosure-controls.md) — Disclosure controls reveal and hide information and functionality related to specific controls or views.
- [Labels](hig/labels.md) — A label is a static piece of text that people can read and often copy, but not edit.
- [Lists and tables](hig/lists-and-tables.md) — Lists and tables present data in one or more columns of rows.
- [Lockups](hig/lockups.md) — Lockups combine multiple separate views into a single, interactive unit.
- [Outline views](hig/outline-views.md) — An outline view presents hierarchical data in a scrolling list of cells that are organized into columns and rows.
- [Split views](hig/split-views.md) — A split view manages the presentation of multiple adjacent panes of content, each of which can contain a variety of components, including tables, collections, images, and custom views.
- [Tab views](hig/tab-views.md) — A tab view presents multiple mutually exclusive panes of content in the same area, which people can switch between using a tabbed control.

### Menus And Actions  (`hig/menus-and-actions.md`)

- [Activity views](hig/activity-views.md) — An activity view — often called a  — presents a range of tasks that people can perform in the current context.
- [Buttons](hig/buttons.md) — A button initiates an instantaneous action.
- [Context menus](hig/context-menus.md) — A context menu provides access to functionality that’s directly related to an item, without cluttering the interface.
- [Dock menus](hig/dock-menus.md) — On a Mac, people can secondary click an app’s or game’s icon in the Dock to reveal a Dock menu, which presents both system-provided and custom items.
- [Edit menus](hig/edit-menus.md) — An edit menu lets people make changes to selected content in the current view, in addition to offering related commands like Copy, Select, Translate, and Look Up.
- [Home Screen quick actions](hig/home-screen-quick-actions.md) — Home Screen quick actions give people a way to perform app-specific actions from the Home Screen.
- [Menus](hig/menus.md) — A menu reveals its options when people interact with it, making it a space-efficient way to present commands in your app or game.
- [Ornaments](hig/ornaments.md) — In visionOS, an ornament presents controls and information related to a window, without crowding or obscuring the window’s contents.
- [Pop-up buttons](hig/pop-up-buttons.md) — A pop-up button displays a menu of mutually exclusive options.
- [Pull-down buttons](hig/pull-down-buttons.md) — A pull-down button displays a menu of items or actions that directly relate to the button’s purpose.
- [The menu bar](hig/the-menu-bar.md) — On a Mac or an iPad, the menu bar at the top of the screen displays the top-level menus in your app or game.
- [Toolbars](hig/toolbars.md) — A toolbar provides convenient access to frequently used commands, controls, navigation, and search.

### Navigation And Search  (`hig/navigation-and-search.md`)

- [Path controls](hig/path-controls.md) — A path control shows the file system path of a selected file or folder.
- [Search fields](hig/search-fields.md) — A search field lets people search a collection of content for specific terms they enter.
- [Sidebars](hig/sidebars.md) — A sidebar appears on the leading side of a view and lets people navigate between areas of your app or top-level collections of content, like folders and playlists.
- [Tab bars](hig/tab-bars.md) — A tab bar lets people navigate between top-level sections of your app.
- [Token fields](hig/token-fields.md) — A token field is a type of text field that can convert text into  that are easy to select and manipulate.

### Presentation  (`hig/presentation.md`)

- [Action sheets](hig/action-sheets.md) — An action sheet is a modal view that presents choices related to an action people initiate.
- [Alerts](hig/alerts.md) — An alert gives people critical information they need right away.
- [Page controls](hig/page-controls.md) — A page control displays a row of indicator images, each of which represents a page in a flat list.
- [Panels](hig/panels.md) — In a macOS app, a panel typically floats above other open windows providing supplementary controls, options, or information related to the active window or current selection.
- [Popovers](hig/popovers.md) — A popover is a transient view that appears above other content when people click or tap a control or interactive area.
- [Scroll views](hig/scroll-views.md) — A scroll view lets people view content that’s larger than the view’s boundaries by moving the content vertically or horizontally.
- [Sheets](hig/sheets.md) — A sheet helps people perform a scoped task that’s closely related to their current context.
- [Windows](hig/windows.md) — A window presents UI views and components in your app or game.

### Selection And Input  (`hig/selection-and-input.md`)

- [Color wells](hig/color-wells.md) — A color well lets people adjust the color of text, shapes, guides, and other onscreen elements.
- [Combo boxes](hig/combo-boxes.md) — A combo box combines a text field with a pull-down button in a single control.
- [Digit entry views](hig/digit-entry-views.md) — A digit entry view fills the entire screen and prompts people to enter a series of digits, like a PIN, using a digit-specific keyboard.
- [Image wells](hig/image-wells.md) — An image well is an editable version of an image view.
- [Pickers](hig/pickers.md) — A picker displays one or more scrollable lists of distinct values that people can choose from.
- [Segmented controls](hig/segmented-controls.md) — A segmented control is a linear set of two or more segments, each of which functions as a button.
- [Sliders](hig/sliders.md) — A slider is a horizontal track with a control, called a thumb, that people can adjust between a minimum and maximum value.
- [Steppers](hig/steppers.md) — A stepper is a two-segment control that people use to increase or decrease an incremental value.
- [Text fields](hig/text-fields.md) — A text field is a rectangular area in which people enter or edit small, specific pieces of text.
- [Toggles](hig/toggles.md) — A toggle lets people choose between a pair of opposing states, like on and off, using a different appearance to indicate each state.
- [Virtual keyboards](hig/virtual-keyboards.md) — On devices without physical keyboards, the system offers various types of virtual keyboards people can use to enter data.

### Status  (`hig/status.md`)

- [Activity rings](hig/activity-rings.md) — Activity rings show an individual’s daily progress toward Move, Exercise, and Stand goals.
- [Gauges](hig/gauges.md) — A gauge displays a specific numerical value within a range of values.
- [Progress indicators](hig/progress-indicators.md) — Progress indicators let people know that your app isn’t stalled while it loads content or performs lengthy operations.
- [Rating indicators](hig/rating-indicators.md) — A rating indicator uses a series of horizontally arranged graphical symbols — by default, stars — to communicate a ranking level.

### System Experiences  (`hig/system-experiences.md`)

- [App Shortcuts](hig/app-shortcuts.md) — An App Shortcut gives people access to your app’s key functions or content throughout the system.
- [Complications](hig/complications.md) — A complication displays timely, relevant information on the watch face, where people can view it each time they raise their wrist.
- [Controls](hig/controls.md) — A control provides quick access to a feature of your app from Control Center, the Lock Screen, or the Action button.
- [Live Activities](hig/live-activities.md) — A Live Activity lets people track the progress of an activity, event, or task at a glance.
- [Notifications](hig/notifications.md) — A notification gives people timely, high-value information they can understand at a glance.
- [Snippets](hig/snippets.md) — When someone performs a task with Siri or an App Shortcut, a snippet shows the result or asks for confirmation.
- [Status bars](hig/status-bars.md) — A status bar appears along the upper edge of the screen and displays information about the device’s current state, like the time, cellular carrier, and battery level.
- [Top Shelf](hig/top-shelf.md) — The Apple TV Home Screen provides an area called Top Shelf, which showcases your content in a rich, engaging way while also giving people access to their favorite apps in the Dock.
- [Watch faces](hig/watch-faces.md) — A watch face is a view that people choose as their primary view in watchOS.
- [Widgets](hig/widgets.md) — A widget provides quick access to essential information and focused interactions from your app or game in additional contexts.

## Inputs

- [Action button](hig/action-button.md) — The Action button gives people quick access to their favorite features on supported iPhone and Apple Watch models.
- [Apple Pencil and Scribble](hig/apple-pencil-and-scribble.md) — Apple Pencil helps make drawing, handwriting, and marking effortless and natural, in addition to performing well as a pointer and UI interaction tool.
- [Camera Control](hig/camera-control.md) — The Camera Control provides direct access to your app’s camera experience.
- [Digital Crown](hig/digital-crown.md) — The Digital Crown is an important hardware input for Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch.
- [Eyes](hig/eyes.md) — In visionOS, people look at a virtual object to identify it as a target they can interact with.
- [Focus and selection](hig/focus-and-selection.md) — Focus helps people visually confirm the object that their interaction targets.
- [Game controls](hig/game-controls.md) — Precise, intuitive game controls enhance gameplay and can increase a player’s immersion in the game.
- [Gestures](hig/gestures.md) — A gesture is a physical motion that a person uses to directly affect an object in an app or game on their device.
- [Gyroscope and accelerometer](hig/gyro-and-accelerometer.md) — On-device gyroscopes and accelerometers can supply data about a device’s movement in the physical world.
- [Keyboards](hig/keyboards.md) — A physical keyboard can be an essential input device for entering text, playing games, controlling apps, and more.
- [Nearby interactions](hig/nearby-interactions.md) — Nearby interactions support on-device experiences that integrate the presence of people and objects in the nearby environment.
- [Pointing devices](hig/pointing-devices.md) — People can use a pointing device like a trackpad or mouse to navigate the interface and initiate actions.
- [Remotes](hig/remotes.md) — The Siri Remote is the primary input method for Apple TV, helping people feel connected to onscreen content from across the room.

## Technologies

- [AirPlay](hig/airplay.md) — AirPlay lets people stream media content wirelessly from iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and tvOS devices to Apple TV, HomePod, and TVs and speakers that support AirPlay.
- [Always On](hig/always-on.md) — On devices that include the Always On display, the system can continue to display an app’s interface when people suspend their interactions with the device.
- [App Clips](hig/app-clips.md) — An App Clip is a lightweight version of your app or game that provides an on-the-go or demo experience that’s instantly available.
- [Apple Pay](hig/apple-pay.md) — Apple Pay is a secure, easy way to make payments for physical goods and services, donations, and subscriptions in apps and in any browser.
- [Augmented reality](hig/augmented-reality.md) — Augmented reality (or AR) lets you deliver immersive, engaging experiences that seamlessly blend virtual objects with the real world.
- [CareKit](hig/carekit.md) — People can use CareKit apps to manage care plans related to a chronic illness like diabetes, recover from an injury or surgery, or achieve health and wellness goals.
- [CarPlay](hig/carplay.md) — CarPlay lets people get directions, make calls, send and receive messages, listen to music, and more from their car’s built-in display, all while staying focused on the road.
- [Game Center](hig/game-center.md) — Game Center is Apple’s social gaming network, which lets players track their progress and connect with friends across Apple platforms, and boosts the discovery of your game across players’ devices.
- [Generative AI](hig/generative-ai.md) — Generative AI empowers you to enhance your app or game with dynamic content and offer intelligent features that unlock new levels of creativity, connection, and productivity.
- [HealthKit](hig/healthkit.md) — HealthKit is the central repository for health and fitness data in iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS.
- [HomeKit](hig/homekit.md) — HomeKit lets people securely control connected accessories in their homes using Siri or the Home app on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac.
- [iCloud](hig/icloud.md) — iCloud is a service that lets people seamlessly access the content they care about — photos, videos, documents, and more — from any device, without performing explicit synchronization.
- [ID Verifier](hig/id-verifier.md) — ID Verifier lets your iPhone app read mobile IDs in person without requiring external hardware.
- [iMessage apps and stickers](hig/imessage-apps-and-stickers.md) — An iMessage app can help people share content, collaborate, and even play games with others in a conversation; stickers are images that people can use to decorate a conversation.
- [In-app purchase](hig/in-app-purchase.md) — People can use in-app purchase to pay for virtual goods — like premium content, digital goods, and subscriptions — securely within your app.
- [Live Photos](hig/live-photos.md) — Live Photos lets people capture favorite memories in a sound- and motion-rich interactive experience that adds vitality to traditional still photos.
- [Mac Catalyst](hig/mac-catalyst.md) — When you use Mac Catalyst to create a Mac version of your iPad app, you give people the opportunity to enjoy the experience in a new environment.
- [Machine learning](hig/machine-learning.md) — Machine learning enables apps and games to learn from data and usage patterns, letting you improve existing experiences and create engaging new ones.
- [Maps](hig/maps.md) — A map displays outdoor or indoor geographical data in your app or on your website.
- [NFC](hig/nfc.md) — Near-field communication (NFC) allows devices within a few centimeters of each other to exchange information wirelessly.
- [Photo editing](hig/photo-editing.md) — Photo-editing extensions let people modify photos and videos within the Photos app by applying filters or making other changes.
- [ResearchKit](hig/researchkit.md) — A research app lets people everywhere participate in important medical research studies.
- [SharePlay](hig/shareplay.md) — SharePlay lets people experience activities together from anywhere, whether they’re watching a movie, playing a game, or sketching on a whiteboard.
- [ShazamKit](hig/shazamkit.md) — ShazamKit supports audio recognition by matching an audio sample against the ShazamKit catalog or a custom audio catalog.
- [Sign in with Apple](hig/sign-in-with-apple.md) — Sign in with Apple provides a fast, private way to sign into apps and websites, giving people a consistent experience they can trust and the convenience of not having to remember multiple accounts and passwords.
- [Siri](hig/siri.md) — People use Siri to help them with the things they need to find, know, or do every day.
- [Tap to Pay on iPhone](hig/tap-to-pay-on-iphone.md) — Tap to Pay on iPhone lets merchants accept contactless payments using an app on their iPhone, without having to connect external hardware.
- [VoiceOver](hig/voiceover.md) — VoiceOver is a screen reader that lets people experience your app’s interface without needing to see the screen.
- [Wallet](hig/wallet.md) — Wallet helps people securely store their credit and debit cards, driver’s license or state ID, transit cards, event tickets, keys, and more on iPhone and Apple Watch.
