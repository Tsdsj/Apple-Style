This notice is example text for a sample site. It is not legal advice. Replace it with the notice that fits your own product and jurisdiction.

What we collect

When you create a workspace we keep your name, your work email and the boards you create. When you send a message through the contact form we keep the message and the fields you filled in.

How we use it

We use the information to run the workspace you asked for, to answer your messages and, if you agree, to send the blog email. We do not use board content to train models.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses a session cookie to keep you signed in to the app. The marketing pages do not set tracking cookies in this example. A theme choice, if you make one, is stored on your device only.

Who we share it with

We share data with the hosting provider that runs the service and with the email provider that sends your confirmations. Each has a contract that limits its use of the data.

Your choices

You can export or delete your boards at any time from the settings. To ask for a copy of your data, or to close your account, contact us with the address you use for the workspace.
