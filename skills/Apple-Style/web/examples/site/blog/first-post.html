Start with a plan

A plan is a list of work with an order and an owner. Most teams already have the first part. The second part is what makes the list useful, and it is the part that is easiest to skip.

List the work before you order it

Write each piece of work as a card whose title starts with a verb. Do not sort the cards yet. Sorting too early hides the things nobody has thought of. When the list stops growing, move on.

Then keep the reasons next to each card, as described in notes that are still useful in March.

A plan that nobody can read is not a plan.

Give every card one owner

A card with two owners has none. Put one name on it, even when several people help. The name is the person who answers when the card is late.

Keep a copy

The export command writes the board to a file, so the plan survives a change of tool:

ferrule export --board "Launch week" --format csv
