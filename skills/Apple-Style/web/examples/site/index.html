Questions

Can we move from another tool?

Yes. Import a CSV export from most planning tools, then check the column names on the first board.

Who can see a board?

Only the people you invite. Each board shows its member list at the top, and you can remove someone at any time.

Does Ferrule work without a connection?

You can read and edit cards offline. Changes are sent the next time the device connects.

How do I cancel?

Open billing in the settings and choose Cancel. Your boards stay readable on the Starter plan.

Where is my data stored?

In data centres inside the region you choose when you create your workspace. The privacy notice explains the details.
