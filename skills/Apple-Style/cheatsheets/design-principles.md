# Apple design principles (HIG, reintroduced June 2026)

Source: `Apple-Style-HIG/reference/hig/design-principles.md`. Use these to break ties between competing options; state which principle drove a decision.

| Principle | One line | Apply by asking |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** — make something meaningful | Identify what matters most to the people you design for; make those things great | What is this screen *for*? Does every element serve it? |
| **Agency** — let people do things their own way | Stay out of the way, allow exploration, make mistakes recoverable | Can the user skip/escape flows? Is there undo? Are they informed? |
| **Responsibility** — act in people's best interest | Transparent about what the product does and why; protect data | Is the permission rationale clear? Do we collect only what's needed? |
| **Familiarity** — build on what people know | Real-world and platform conventions, consistent visuals & interactions, clear feedback | Would a user of other Apple apps know how this works instantly? |
| **Flexibility** — adapt to diverse contexts and needs | Design for everyone, preserve context across platforms, support many input methods, give each platform intention | Does it work with keyboard, voice, pointer, touch; at AX5; in RTL; on Mac/iPad/iPhone? |
| **Simplicity** — be clear and direct | Include just what's necessary; be concise; establish hierarchy | What can be removed? Is the primary action obvious? |
| **Craft** — care about every detail | Quality sets the tone; experiment and iterate; maintain over time | Are alignment, radii, motion, copy all deliberate? Tested on device? |
| **Delight** — make it human | Identify the emotion; create defining moments; delight ≠ decoration; consider the whole | What should it feel like? Does the "delight" ever get in the way of the task? |

## Platform mindset (from *Designing for iOS/iPadOS/macOS/…*)
- **iPhone**: focused, vertical, one-handed reach — primary actions bottom-reachable; tab bar; large titles; full-screen content.
- **iPad**: bridges phone and Mac; sidebars, multi-column split views, pointer + keyboard, multitasking/resizable windows, menu bar, drag & drop.
- **Mac**: dense, wide, many windows; menu bar with full command set and shortcuts; toolbars; inspectors; precise pointer; small controls acceptable.
- **watchOS**: glanceable, seconds-long interactions, Digital Crown, complications, always-on.
- **tvOS**: 10-foot UI, focus-driven, lockups, Top Shelf, remote/game controller.
- **visionOS**: windows/volumes/spaces on glass, eyes + hands, 60pt targets, depth & ergonomics, keep people grounded.
- One anatomy, many expressions: design the app's structure once and let each platform express it (continuity).
